# V4.6.5 — THRILLER BOSS ARC

## Boss progression
- Level 15: Yellow Thriller First Form is explicitly rendered with the Yellow boss asset; the generic red pre-boss scare no longer appears for this encounter.
- Level 40: Black/Yellow Thriller, 7 lives.
- Level 50: Thriller Boss, 10 lives:
  - Lives 10–8: Red Form
  - Lives 7–5: Black/Yellow Form
  - Lives 4–2: Dual Form
  - Life 1: Werewolf Rage
- Werewolf apparition scares at selected progression levels before the final fight, always with the Thriller laugh.

## Special boss attacks
- Gravedigger Brute: Grave Slam
- Shadow Dancer: Shadow Nova + clones
- Yellow Thriller: Dance Shockwave
- Thriller Brutes: Brute Rush
- Black/Yellow Thriller: Blackout Step + summons
- Red Form: Red Shockwave
- Dual Form: alternates Red Shockwave and Blackout Step
- Werewolf Rage: Werewolf Pounce + runner summons

All specials are telegraphed visually before damage, so the player can dodge instead of merely tanking contact damage.
